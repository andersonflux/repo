
🐱 Electrum-MONA — Wallet for Monacoin
======================================

**Electrum-MONA** is a streamlined desktop wallet built specifically for the Japanese cryptocurrency **Monacoin (MONA)**.  
Enjoy full control of your funds, fast transaction processing, and strong security — without downloading the full blockchain.

* * *

🎯 Why Use Electrum-MONA?
-------------------------

* ✅ Instant startup with SPV (Simplified Payment Verification)
    
* 🔐 Private keys remain under your control
    
* 💻 Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux
    
* 🔌 Supports hardware wallets (Trezor, Ledger)
    
* 🇯🇵 Trusted by the Japanese crypto community
